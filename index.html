About Me

For 25 years, I've been deeply involved in the world of Quality Assurance. My journey has been one of meticulous attention to detail and a steadfast commitment to delivering robust, high-quality software. While my expertise lies in manual testing methodologies, my experience spans the entire software development lifecycle, ensuring that every release meets the highest standards of functionality, usability, and performance. My extensive experience has honed a unique set of skills crucial for effective QA: In-depth Test Planning and Strategy: I excel at crafting comprehensive test plans that cover all aspects of an application. This involves understanding business requirements, identifying potential risks, and designing test cases that thoroughly validate every feature. Expert Manual Test Execution: I possess a keen eye for detail and a methodical approach to executing test cases. I'm adept at uncovering bugs that automated scripts might miss, focusing on user experience and edge cases. Defect Identification and Reporting: My ability to clearly and concisely document defects is a significant asset. I ensure that each bug report is detailed, reproducible, and provides developers with all the necessary information to resolve issues efficiently. Cross-functional Collaboration: I've consistently worked closely with development teams, product managers, and stakeholders to foster a shared understanding of quality and to ensure alignment on release goals. User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Coordination: I have a strong track record of facilitating and supporting UAT, ensuring that end-users can validate the software's readiness for deployment. Exploratory Testing Mastery: Beyond scripted tests, I leverage exploratory testing to uncover unexpected issues and to gain a deep understanding of the application's behavior in real-world scenarios. Domain Knowledge Accumulation: Over two and a half decades, I've gained valuable insights into various industries and application domains, allowing me to approach testing with a more informed and context-aware perspective. While the landscape of testing has evolved, the fundamental principles of rigorous manual verification remain paramount. My career has been dedicated to upholding these principles, ensuring that the software I've helped validate has consistently met and exceeded expectations.

In my free time, I enjoy being out in nature, spending time with family & friends, playing board games, pickleball, and trying new restaurants.
