About Me

For 25 years, I've been deeply involved in the world of Quality Assurance. My journey has been one of meticulous attention to detail and a steadfast commitment to delivering robust, high-quality software. While my expertise lies in manual testing methodologies, my experience spans the entire software development lifecycle, ensuring that every release meets the highest standards of functionality, usability, and performance. My extensive experience has honed a unique set of skills crucial for effective QA.

In-depth Test Planning and Strategy: I excel at crafting comprehensive test plans that cover all aspects of an application. This involves understanding business requirements, identifying potential risks, and designing test cases that thoroughly validate every feature.

Expert Manual Test Execution: I possess a keen eye for detail and a methodical approach to executing test cases. I'm adept at uncovering bugs that automated scripts might miss, focusing on user experience and edge cases.

Domain Knowledge Accumulation: For two and a half decades, I've gained valuable insights into various industries and application domains, allowing me to approach testing with a more informed and context-aware perspective.

I strive to lead QA teams by defining quality strategies, establishing testing standards and processes, managing resources and workload, developing team capabilities, and reporting on quality metrics to stakeholders. I create an environment where quality is everyone's responsibility.

While the landscape of testing has evolved, the fundamental principles of rigorous manual verification remain paramount. My career has been dedicated to upholding these principles, ensuring that the software I've helped validate has consistently met and exceeded expectations.

In my free time, I enjoy being out in nature, spending time with family & friends, playing board games, pickleball, and trying new restaurants.
